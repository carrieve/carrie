## What it is

GetCarrieAI is a preference capture system for anyone who works with an AI assistant. Fill out a short form, download a profile file, upload it to a Claude Project — and Claude knows your preferences permanently.

No installation. No plugin. No code. Works on your phone.

---

## Who it's for

- **Busy professionals** — set up your travel, food, and logistics preferences once. Ask Claude anything.
- **Executive Assistants** — store every traveler's preferences. Instant answers while you're booking — no hunting through notes.
- **Small business owners** — set up your business profile. Claude knows your context and helps with emails and admin.

---

## How it works

**1. Fill out your profile**
Go to [getcarrieai.com](https://getcarrieai.com) and fill out one or more forms. Takes 2–5 minutes.

**2. Upload to a Claude Project**
Sign up free at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) → Projects → New Project → upload your file. Claude now knows your preferences in every conversation.

**3. Just ask**
- *"What are my flight preferences?"*
- *"What's my TSA PreCheck number?"*
- *"What hotel chain do I use and what's my loyalty number?"*
- *"Draft a follow-up email from these meeting notes."*

---

## Forms

All forms are live at [getcarrieai.com](https://getcarrieai.com):

| Form | What it captures |
|------|-----------------|
| **Quick Profile** | Name, airline, seat, hotel loyalty, coffee, food — 5 questions |
| **Travel Preferences** | Full flight, hotel, ground transport, loyalty programs, dietary |
| **Food & Coffee** | Coffee order, restaurants, dietary restrictions, delivery address |
| **Small Business** | Business basics, communication style, admin pain points, tools |
| **Import Notes** | Paste existing notes, convert to structured profile |
| **Meeting Notes** | Paste a transcript, get action items + follow-up email |

Each form downloads a JSON file. Upload that file to your Claude Project.
