Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

The short version: your profile data stays on your device. We don't store it on our servers. When you talk to Carrie, your messages go to Anthropic's API and are subject to their privacy policy.

What we collect

When you use GetCarrieAI, here's what happens with your information:

  • Profile data you enter during onboarding — name, preferences, travel details, communication style — is built into a JSON file entirely in your browser. It is never sent to our servers. You download it and keep it.
  • Conversation messages — when you type or speak to Carrie on this site, your messages are sent to Anthropic's API (api.anthropic.com) via our serverless function. We do not log, store, or read your conversation content.
  • Email address — if you sign up for our mailing list, your email is stored by Netlify Forms. We use it only to send product updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not store your profile data on any server
  • We do not read or log your conversations with Carrie
  • We do not sell data to third parties
  • We do not use tracking pixels or behavioral advertising
  • We do not collect passport numbers or government ID — this feature is disabled until further notice

Your profile file

Your profile is a JSON file that lives on your device. You control it completely — you can read it, edit it, delete it, or upload it to Claude.ai. When you upload it to a Claude Project, it is governed by Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

Anthropic API

Conversations on this site use the Anthropic API. Your messages are processed by Anthropic in accordance with their Privacy Policy and Usage Policy. We pass your messages to their API and return the response — nothing more.

Cookies and local storage

We use browser local storage only to remember your voice preference (on/off). We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising.

Children

GetCarrieAI is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.
