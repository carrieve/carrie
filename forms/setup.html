Your Assistant is Almost Ready

You're two minutes away from an assistant that remembers everything about you. Follow these steps and Carrie will know your preferences in every conversation — without you ever having to explain again.

1

Go to claude.ai and create a Project

Sign up free at claude.ai → click Projects in the sidebar → New Project. Give it any name — "My Assistant" works great.

2

Upload your profile file

Inside your new Project, look for Add content or the upload icon. Upload the carrie-profile.json file you just downloaded. Claude will read it automatically.

3

Copy this into your Project instructions

In your Project, click Edit instructions (or the pencil icon). Paste the block below. This tells Claude exactly how to be your assistant.

You are my personal AI executive assistant. My profile file contains everything about me — my preferences, my work, my schedule, and my communication style. Always read my profile before answering. Never ask me for information that's already in my profile. When I say "Good morning", give me a brief, warm daily briefing based on what you know about me — what's on deck, anything flagged, anything I should know. Keep it tight. When I ask you to write something, match my voice and style exactly as described in my profile. When I ask about travel, food, scheduling, or logistics — use my preferences from my profile without me having to repeat them. Be warm, efficient, and direct. You're a trusted colleague, not a customer service bot. One or two sentences is almost always enough unless I ask for more. If I ask you to remember something new, tell me to add it to my profile so it sticks permanently.
4

Say "Good morning"

That's it. Type Good morning in your Project and watch Carrie go. She already knows everything.

Example Good Morning response:

"Good morning! You've got a 2pm call with the team and Sarah needs her travel booked for Thursday. The vendor email from yesterday is still waiting on a response — want me to draft something? What do you want to tackle first?"
