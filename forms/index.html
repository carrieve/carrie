Step-by-step setup

1
Create a free Claude account
Go to claude.ai and sign up. Free. Takes 2 minutes. Download the app on your phone too — search "Claude" in the App Store or Google Play.
2
Fill out your profile
Use the ⚡ Quick Profile above (5 questions, 2 min) or fill out the full Travel or Food form. Download the file when you're done.
3
Create a Claude Project and upload your file
In Claude, tap Projects → New Project. Upload your profile file. Claude now knows your preferences permanently — in every conversation in that project.
4
Optional: Install Claude Code for hands-on booking
Want Claude to actually book flights and hotels? Download Claude Code (Mac/Windows/Linux) and upgrade to Claude Pro ($20/mo). Then just ask: "Book me a flight to NYC Tuesday."
