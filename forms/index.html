How it works

1

Collect — Send a form link

Share a form link with your traveler. They fill it out once — seat preferences, loyalty numbers, TSA PreCheck, exact coffee order, dietary restrictions, delivery address. Everything.

2

Save — Drop the file in place

When they submit, a profile file downloads. Save it to a folder or upload it to a Claude Project. That's it — no database, no setup, no IT ticket.

3

Ask — Just tell Claude what you need

Next time you need to book travel or order food, just ask your AI assistant. It looks up the profile automatically and takes care of the rest.
