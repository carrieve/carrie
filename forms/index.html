The obvious question

"Why not just type my preferences into ChatGPT?"

Most people would. They'd write "I like aisle seats and Marriott" and call it done.

The difference is the questions.

GetCarrieAI asks what you wouldn't think to mention. Who always gets on your exec's calendar — no questions asked. What their partner's birthday gift should actually be. What the FBO is at Teterboro. What a decline email sounds like in their voice. What focus time is actually protecting so you can explain it when someone wants that hour.

That knowledge doesn't live in a document. It lives in one person's head — until they leave, you start a new job, or someone new joins and has to learn it all over again.

A structured file fixes that. Fill it out once. Share it with your backup. Hand it to the next EA. Upload it to any AI. The institutional knowledge travels with the role, not the person.
