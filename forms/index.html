How it works

1

Set up your profile once

Fill out your preferences once — seat preferences, loyalty numbers, TSA PreCheck, coffee order, dietary restrictions, delivery address. Download the file when you're done.

2

Upload to a Claude Project

Go to claude.ai → Projects → New Project. Upload your profile file. That's it — Claude now knows your preferences. No app to install, no account to create.

3

Just ask — like texting a colleague

"Book me a flight to NYC Tuesday, nonstop aisle." "Order my usual coffee." "Find a dinner spot in Chicago." Claude knows your preferences and handles the rest.

💡
You need a Claude account — it's free to start
Go to claude.ai and sign up. The free plan lets you create Projects and upload files. Claude Pro ($20/mo) gives you more usage if you're supporting multiple executives.
