# GetCarrieAI — Loom Demo Script
**Runtime: ~60–90 seconds | No account needed | No audio required (captions work)**

---

## Setup (before you hit record)

1. Open a fresh browser window — no other tabs visible
2. Go to: `getcarrieai.com/demo-load.html`
3. Click **"Load demo data → open kit"** — this fills all 5 sections with a fictional exec (Marcus Chen, CEO)
4. You'll land on kit.html with everything already filled in
5. Start Loom **now** — you're ready

---

## The Recording

### Scene 1 — The homepage (10 sec)
- Navigate to `getcarrieai.com`
- Let it sit for 2 seconds so viewers can read the headline
- **Say / caption:** *"This is GetCarrieAI — you fill out the kit once, and your AI knows your executive permanently."*
- Click **"Start your onboarding kit →"**

---

### Scene 2 — The kit, fully loaded (20 sec)
- You land on kit.html — all 5 sections show green checkmarks and 100% progress
- **Say / caption:** *"Each section covers a different part of your executive's world — profile, team, travel, food, household. You can do them in any order, one at a time."*
- Scroll slowly so viewers see all 5 green items
- Scroll down to the full briefing text area

---

### Scene 3 — The briefing (15 sec)
- The full briefing is assembled and ready in the text area
- **Say / caption:** *"GetCarrieAI packages everything into one briefing. You copy it once and paste it into your AI."*
- Click **"Copy full briefing"** — button turns green and says "Copied!"

---

### Scene 4 — Paste into Claude (20 sec)
- Open a new tab, go to `claude.ai`
- Start a new conversation
- Paste the briefing (Cmd+V / Ctrl+V)
- **Say / caption:** *"Paste it in. That's it. Claude now knows your executive."*
- Hit send — wait for Claude to confirm it read the briefing

---

### Scene 5 — Prove it works (15 sec)
- Type: **"What hotel chain does Marcus prefer, and what's his loyalty number?"**
- Claude responds with: Marriott, Titanium Elite, Bonvoy #987654321
- **Say / caption:** *"Your AI now knows everything — and it will for every conversation going forward."*

---

### Scene 6 — Close (5 sec)
- Cut back to `getcarrieai.com`
- **Say / caption:** *"Free. No account. Works with Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and more. getcarrieai.com"*
- Stop recording

---

## Tips
- Keep your browser zoom at 100% so text is readable
- Hide bookmarks bar before recording (Cmd+Shift+B on Mac)
- If Claude asks a clarifying question in Scene 5, just retype: *"Based on the briefing I just gave you, what hotel does Marcus prefer?"*
- The demo data can be cleared anytime at `getcarrieai.com/demo-load.html` → "Clear demo data"
