How to use this library

Copy any prompt, open Claude inside your Project (not a regular chat — your Project), and paste it in. Replace anything in [brackets] with your actual details.

Claude already knows your preferences from your uploaded profile — these prompts tell it what to do with them.

Travel

✈️ Flights & Airports

Use these when you're planning a trip or need to look something up fast.

"What are my flight preferences?"

Gets Claude to read your profile and summarize your airline, seat, and class preferences in one shot.

"Find me a nonstop flight from [city] to [city] on [date] — use my preferences."

Claude will reference your preferred airline, seat, and class and search accordingly.

"What's my TSA PreCheck number?"

Instant lookup from your profile. No digging through emails.

"What's my frequent flyer number for [airline]?"

Works for any airline you've added to your profile.

"I'm flying to [city] next [day]. What do I need to know or prep?"

Claude will remind you of your preferences and help you think through logistics.

Travel

🏨 Hotels

Look up loyalty info or get hotel recommendations in seconds.

"What hotel chain do I prefer and what's my loyalty number?"

Quick reference for when you're booking at the front desk or on the phone.

"Find a [Marriott / Hilton / Hyatt] in [city] for [check-in date] to [check-out date]."

Claude will use your loyalty program and room preferences from your profile.

"What are my hotel room preferences?"

Pulls your bed type, floor preference, and any room notes from your profile.

"I'm checking into a hotel. What should I ask for at the front desk based on my preferences?"

Great for when you're standing at check-in and want to make sure you don't forget anything.

Food & Coffee

☕ Orders & Restaurants

Never explain your order again.

"What's my usual coffee order?"

Fast lookup. Perfect for when someone else is making a coffee run.

"What are my dietary restrictions?"

Instant answer for restaurant menus, catering forms, or travel situations.

"Find a good [cuisine] restaurant near [location] that works for my dietary needs."

Claude will factor in your restrictions and preferences when searching.

"I'm ordering from [restaurant]. What should I get based on my usual preferences?"

Good for unfamiliar menus when you want a recommendation that fits your taste.

"What's my delivery address?"

Pulls your saved delivery address from your food profile.

Scheduling & Communications

📅 Calendar & Email

Draft faster. Schedule smarter.

"Schedule a [30 / 60]-minute meeting with [name] next week. My availability is [times]."

Claude can draft the invite or suggest times and write the outreach message.

"Draft a follow-up email to [name] after our meeting today. We discussed: [topic]. Action items: [list]."

Claude writes it in a professional, warm tone — ready to copy and send.

"Process these meeting notes and give me action items: [paste notes]"

Claude extracts who's doing what and by when. Works with rough notes or full transcripts.

"Draft a professional email declining this meeting: [paste invite]"

Warm, concise, no awkward wording. Just paste the invite and Claude handles the reply.

"Write a thank-you email to [name] after [context]."

Claude keeps it genuine and specific — not a generic template.

For Executive Assistants

💼 Supporting Executives

If you've uploaded profiles for multiple travelers, use their name in the prompt and Claude will pull the right one.

"What are [name]'s travel preferences?"

Full summary of their airline, seat, hotel, loyalty programs — everything in one go.

"What's [name]'s TSA PreCheck or Global Entry number?"

Instant lookup while you're on the phone with an airline or filling out a booking form.

"What hotel does [name] prefer? What's their loyalty number?"

Never ask the same question twice.

"Book a flight for [name] to [city] on [date]. Use their preferences."

Claude will pull their profile and walk you through the search with their preferences applied.

"Does [name] have any dietary restrictions I should know about for this event?"

Great before catering orders, restaurant bookings, or team events.
